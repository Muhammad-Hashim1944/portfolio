HM Ismail & Sons: A Minimal Shopify Rebuild

HM Ismail & Sons sells bathroom, sanitary ware, and hardware fittings. The founder, Ahmad, had already built a loyal customer base through word of mouth and local trust, but his online store didn't carry that same weight. It felt cluttered and generic, the kind of site that could belong to almost any hardware seller in the country.

Ahmad needed something simpler. A minimal, professional Shopify store that let his products speak for themselves instead of competing with banners and pop-ups for attention.

The results showed up fast. In the month after the changes went live, HM Ismail & Sons brought in PKR 158,473 in total sales, a 440 percent increase over the previous month.

Sands Drop: Building a UAE Store From Zero

Sands Drop started with nothing. No existing customer base, no catalogue, no infrastructure of any kind. It was a UAE-targeted dropshipping store, launched and managed entirely remotely.

The work covered everything from the ground up, from listing and optimizing the full product catalogue to setting up the Shopify apps the store needed to keep sales moving.

The full month of July 2025 shows what that built. The store pulled in 8,460 sessions, a 129 percent increase. Gross sales reached AED 31,452, up 315 percent, and orders climbed to 329, a 345 percent jump.
