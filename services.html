The Combined Approach

How development and creative strategy work together

Running ads to a confusing store burns your ad budget. Building a clean store without proven ad angles leaves your traffic dry.

1. Market & Angle Research

Find where competitors are weak. Map customer pain points and build testing angles that address specific buyer hesitations.

2. Store Architecture

Build the store layout to reinforce the ad promise. Remove popups and unnecessary apps so customers can evaluate products directly.

3. Test & Refine

Cut products and angles that do not convert. Scale catalogue items and ad creatives that generate profitable volume.
