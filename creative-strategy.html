THE SYSTEM

The Creative Yield Engine™

A research-led system for turning customer insight into testable ad creative.

01
Phase 01

Deep Audience Research

Analyze customer reviews, forum discussions, and competitor feedback to uncover real customer frustrations, unmet needs, previous disappointments, and desired outcomes.

Customer Insight Note

“Tried 4 serums before this... nobody talks about the quiet panic of checking the drain every morning.”

Source: Verified Forum & Review Mining
02
Phase 02

Pain Point Mapping

Organize customer frustrations by priority and connect each problem to a relevant product benefit and supporting evidence.

Pain Point Morning hair fall anxiety
Product Benefit Follicle root fortification
Evidence / Proof 6-oil + cold-pressed herb matrix
03
Phase 03

Hook & Angle Engineering

Develop distinct creative angles and multiple hooks designed to test different ways of communicating a product's value.

Angle A (Emotional): “The panic doesn't happen in public...”
Angle B (Contrast): “Stop buying 5 products for 1 routine.”
Angle C (Demo/Proof): “Why this 6-oil matrix absorbs in 45s.”
04
Phase 04

Objection & Proof Engineering

Identify purchase objections and match them with relevant product demonstrations, customer feedback, or substantiated evidence.

Buyer Objection

“Will this leave my scalp greasy or require multiple washes?”

Substantiated Proof

Side-by-side absorption macro footage + zero-residue rinse demonstration.

THE OUTPUT

A clear creative testing roadmap — not random ad ideas.

Research → Angles → Hooks → Proof → Testable variations

Independent Creative Audit

Pristine by Shaheera — Ad Script Teardown

Niche: DTC Haircare · Format: Video ad script · No paid engagement
Before — Original Script
"Firstly, you can't do anything about the hair that fell on the floor, but you can save the 4 remaining hairs. But nothing was working on my hair. And when the hair falls on the floor, no one can understand the anxiety better than me. And the best part is that you don't need any conditioner or serum after these hair oils. And you won't be able to see any frizz anywhere. It doesn't just have 1 oil, it has a 6 oil blend. And then many more herbs are infused in it. And it's such a powerful blend to control your hair fall. This is the best thing if you want your hair not to fall on the floor."
After — Rewritten with the System
[0:00–0:04] HOOK Visual: tight close-up, dim bathroom lighting, no makeup, handheld feel. VO: The panic doesn't happen in public. It happens alone, in the bathroom, at night. [0:04–0:11] Visual: hand running through hair over the sink, water running, strands on fingers. VO: You turn the tap on louder so nobody hears you getting upset over a hairbrush, and you check the drain before you even look at your own face. [0:11–0:18] Visual: sitting on the edge of the tub, holding a fallen strand, looking down. VO: I did this for actual weeks, just sitting there thinking, what now, because I'd already tried like four other things before this one. [0:18–0:25] Visual: product reveal, natural window light, holding the bottle at eye level. VO: What actually changed it for me came down to one bottle, six oils and a full herb blend packed in, not another random pick off a shelf. [0:25–0:32] Visual: demo, applying oil to scalp and roots, close on hands. VO: You don't even need conditioner or serum after it. [0:32–0:38] Visual: back to face, softer lighting than the opening, calmer expression. VO: The shedding slowed down in like two weeks, and by week six I actually stopped checking the drain every morning. [0:38–0:40] CLOSE / CTA Visual: product on the counter, text overlay appears. VO: If your bathroom's where it happens for you too, this is where it stops. On-screen text: Link in bio
What changed, and why
  • Hook moved from a vague statement about hair loss to a specific, private moment of shame (Hook & Angle Engineering)
  • Pain point now leads the script instead of trailing after product features (Pain Point Mapping)
  • Added timed shot-by-shot visual direction — the original was VO only, with no production structure
  • CTA ties back to the emotional arc opened in the hook, instead of ending on a feature list
